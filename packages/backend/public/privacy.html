1. Introduction

IndusWealth ("we", "us", "our", or the "Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information when you use the IndusWealth mobile application and related services (the "Service").

This Privacy Policy complies with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA, S.C. 2000, c. 5) and applicable provincial privacy legislation.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Email address — Account authentication
  • Password — Stored as a bcrypt hash (never in plaintext)
  • Name — Personalization
  • Date of birth — Age-based financial insights
  • Transaction notes — Personal record-keeping
  • Custom debt entries — Debt payoff calculations

Information from Plaid (Bank Data)

We use Plaid Inc. to connect your bank accounts. Plaid collects your banking credentials directly and we never see or store them. Plaid's handling of your information is governed by the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

  • Account information — names, types, balances, including credit card and loan balances
  • Transaction data — names, amounts, dates, categories

Usage Analytics (Mixpanel)

  • App events (login, screen views, feature usage) tied to a pseudonymous user ID
  • No name, email, or financial data is ever sent to Mixpanel

We do NOT receive your bank login credentials, full account numbers, SIN, credit score, or PINs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Providing the Service

  • Authenticating your identity and managing your account
  • Displaying bank account balances and transaction history
  • Generating spending analytics and category breakdowns
  • Detecting recurring subscriptions and expenses
  • Calculating debt payoff strategies

AI-Powered Features

When generating insights, we send an aggregated, anonymized summary to Google Gemini. We do NOT send your name, email, bank account numbers, or any data that directly identifies you.

4. How We Share Your Information

Third-Party Service Providers

  • Plaid Inc. — Bank account aggregation (United States) — Plaid End User Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini AI — Insight generation (United States)
  • Render.com — Application hosting and database (United States)
  • Mixpanel Inc. — Product usage analytics, pseudonymous events only (United States)
  • Resend — Transactional email such as address verification and password resets (United States)

Processing Outside Canada

The providers above operate in the United States, so your information may be stored and processed outside Canada and may be accessible to foreign courts, law enforcement, or regulatory authorities under the laws of that country. We use only providers that are contractually bound to protect your information to a comparable standard.

We do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Provide your data to data brokers
  • Use your financial data for credit decisions

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary. When you delete your account, we permanently delete all your data including:

  • User profile and credentials
  • All linked bank accounts
  • All transaction records
  • All AI-generated insights
  • All custom debt entries
  • All educational bookmarks

This deletion is irreversible.

6. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit — All data encrypted using TLS/HTTPS
  • Encryption at rest — Plaid tokens and 2FA secrets encrypted with AES-256-GCM
  • Password hashing — bcrypt with cost factor 12
  • JWT authentication — Short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens
  • Two-factor authentication — Optional TOTP 2FA with recovery codes
  • Account lockout — Temporary lockout after repeated failed logins
  • Database security — Parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection
  • Rate limiting — IP-based brute force prevention
  • Input validation — Server-side validation of all input

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Request data portability (JSON/CSV export)
  • Challenge our compliance
