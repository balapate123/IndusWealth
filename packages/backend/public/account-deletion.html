Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your IndusWealth account and all associated data

Option 1 — Delete instantly from the app

If you have the IndusWealth app installed, you can delete your account yourself at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

  1. Open the IndusWealth app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by entering your password.

Your account and all associated data are removed immediately and cannot be recovered.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address associated with your IndusWealth account:

Email: privacy@induswealth.app
Subject: "Account Deletion Request"

We verify the request comes from the account owner and complete the deletion within 30 days, as required by Canada's PIPEDA.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all of your personal data, including:

  • Your profile and login credentials
  • All linked bank accounts and access tokens
  • All transaction records and notes
  • All AI-generated insights
  • All custom debt entries
  • All educational bookmarks
  • Two-factor authentication secrets and recovery codes

This deletion is permanent and irreversible. No personal data is retained after deletion.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove data. Within the app you can:

  • Disconnect a bank — removes that institution's accounts and transactions
  • Remove an individual account — deletes its transaction history

These actions delete the associated data while keeping your IndusWealth account active.

Questions about your data? Contact privacy@induswealth.app
