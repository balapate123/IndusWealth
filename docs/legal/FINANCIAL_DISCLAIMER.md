## 4. Debt Payoff Calculator Disclaimer

The debt payoff features in IndusWealth (snowball and avalanche methods) provide **estimates only**.

### Limitations:

- Calculations are based on the information you provide (balances, APRs, minimum payments) and assume constant interest rates and consistent payments
- **Variable interest rates** are not modelled — if your rates change, actual results will differ
- **Fees and charges** (annual fees, late fees, over-limit fees) are not included in calculations
- **Compounding frequency** may differ from the assumptions used in the calculator
- **Payment timing** (e.g., bi-weekly vs. monthly) can significantly affect actual results
- **Promotional rates** and balance transfer offers are not factored in
- The calculator does not account for potential changes to minimum payment requirements

**Do not rely solely on these calculations when making debt repayment decisions.** Consult with your financial institution or a credit counsellor for a comprehensive debt management plan.

For free, impartial debt counselling in Canada, consider contacting:
- **Credit Counselling Canada**: [https://creditcounsellingcanada.ca](https://creditcounsellingcanada.ca)
- **Financial Consumer Agency of Canada (FCAC)**: [https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency.html)

## 5. Spending Analytics Disclaimer

Spending analytics and category breakdowns are generated based on transaction data received from Plaid and categorized using Plaid's built-in categorization and IndusWealth's AI-enhanced categorization.

### Limitations:

- **Category accuracy**: Transactions may be miscategorized. Merchant names do not always map cleanly to spending categories. AI categorization improves over time but is not perfect.
- **Incomplete data**: Analytics only reflect transactions from linked accounts. Cash transactions, accounts at unlinkable institutions, and transactions from before you linked your account are not included.
- **Timing differences**: Pending transactions may be included or excluded depending on your bank's reporting. Amounts may change when transactions are finalized.
- **Currency**: All amounts are displayed in the currency reported by your financial institution (typically CAD).

## 6. Subscription Detection Disclaimer

The subscription/recurring expense detection feature (Watchdog) uses pattern matching to identify recurring charges.

### Limitations:

- **False positives**: Regular purchases at the same merchant may be incorrectly flagged as subscriptions
- **False negatives**: Some subscriptions may not be detected if payment amounts vary or if the merchant name changes
- **Annual subscriptions**: Subscriptions billed annually may not be detected within the analysis window
- **Shared subscriptions**: The feature cannot determine if a subscription is shared with others
