## 2. PIPEDA Fair Information Principles

Our privacy practices are guided by the ten fair information principles set out in Schedule 1 of PIPEDA:

1. **Accountability** — We are responsible for your personal information in our possession
2. **Identifying Purposes** — We identify why we need your information before or at the time of collection
3. **Consent** — We obtain your knowledge and consent for the collection, use, and disclosure of your information
4. **Limiting Collection** — We collect only the information necessary for the identified purposes
5. **Limiting Use, Disclosure, and Retention** — We use information only for the purposes for which it was collected
6. **Accuracy** — We keep personal information as accurate, complete, and up-to-date as necessary
7. **Safeguards** — We protect personal information with appropriate security measures
8. **Openness** — We make our privacy policies readily available
9. **Individual Access** — You can request access to your personal information
10. **Challenging Compliance** — You can challenge our compliance with these principles

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

### 4.1 Providing the Service

- Authenticating your identity and managing your account
- Displaying your bank account balances and transaction history
- Generating spending analytics and category breakdowns
- Detecting recurring subscriptions and expenses
- Calculating debt payoff strategies
- Delivering educational content through Wealth Academy

### 4.2 AI-Powered Features

- Generating personalized financial insights using Google Gemini AI
- Categorizing transactions using AI merchant classification
- Recommending relevant educational articles based on your financial situation

**What data is sent to Google Gemini AI:**

When generating insights, we send an **aggregated, anonymized summary** of your financial data to Google Gemini. This summary includes:

- Your age (if provided, not your date of birth)
- Account balances by type (checking, savings, credit) — no account names or numbers
- Spending totals by category (e.g., "groceries: $450") — no individual transaction details
- Income estimates (monthly average) — no employer or source names
- Subscription totals (e.g., "Netflix: $16.99/month") — merchant names and amounts only
- Debt summary (total balance, APRs, minimum payments) — no account identifiers
- Cash flow summary (income vs. expenses)

**We do NOT send to Google Gemini:**

- Your name or email address
- Your bank account names, numbers, or institution names
- Individual transaction descriptions or dates
- Your Plaid access tokens or credentials
- Any data that directly identifies you as an individual

### 4.3 Service Improvement

- Monitoring for errors and ensuring service reliability
- Analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve features
- Tracking AI model performance and costs

### 4.4 Communications

- Responding to your support inquiries
- Sending service-related notices (security alerts, maintenance, Terms updates)
- Sending optional financial tips or insights (only with your consent, per CASL)

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

### 7.1 Technical Safeguards

- **Encryption in transit**: All data transmitted between the app, our servers, and third parties is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS
- **Password hashing**: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with a cost factor of 12 and are never stored in plaintext
- **JWT authentication**: Secure token-based authentication for API access
- **Database security**: PostgreSQL database with parameterized queries to prevent SQL injection
- **Rate limiting**: IP-based rate limiting to prevent brute force attacks
- **Input validation**: Server-side validation of all user input
- **Error handling**: Sanitized error responses that do not expose internal system details
- **Request tracing**: Unique request IDs for all API calls to support security auditing

### 7.2 Organizational Safeguards

- Access to production systems is restricted on a need-to-know basis
- Third-party services are reviewed for their security and privacy practices
- Sensitive data (Plaid access tokens) is stored in environment variables separate from application code

### 7.3 Breach Notification

In the event of a security breach involving your personal information, we will:

1. Investigate the breach and take immediate steps to contain it
2. Notify the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (OPC)** as required by PIPEDA's mandatory breach notification provisions (effective November 1, 2018)
3. Notify you directly (via email) if the breach creates a **real risk of significant harm** to you
4. Maintain records of all breaches as required by PIPEDA
5. Notify applicable provincial privacy commissioners where required (e.g., Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec under Law 25)
