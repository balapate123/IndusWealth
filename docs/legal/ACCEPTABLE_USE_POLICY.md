## 1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") defines the acceptable and prohibited uses of the IndusWealth application and related services (the "Service"). This policy is incorporated into and supplements our Terms of Service.

---

## 2. Intended Use

IndusWealth is designed for **personal, non-commercial financial tracking and analysis**. The Service is intended to help individual users:

- Track their personal financial transactions and account balances
- Analyze their personal spending patterns
- Manage and plan debt repayment
- Receive AI-generated financial insights for personal use
- Access educational financial content

---

## 3. Prohibited Uses

You agree not to use the Service for any of the following purposes:

### 3.1 Illegal Activities

- Any activity that violates applicable local, provincial/state, national, or international laws
- Money laundering or proceeds of crime
- Tax evasion or fraud
- Identity theft or impersonation

### 3.2 Unauthorized Access

- Attempting to access another user's account or data
- Attempting to access systems, data, or areas of the Service not intended for you
- Using brute force, credential stuffing, or other automated attack methods against authentication systems
- Exploiting vulnerabilities or security weaknesses in the Service

### 3.3 Service Abuse

- Using automated tools, bots, scripts, or scrapers to interact with the Service
- Generating excessive API requests designed to overload or degrade the Service
- Circumventing rate limits, authentication mechanisms, or other security controls
- Interfering with the Service's availability or performance for other users

### 3.4 Reverse Engineering

- Reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling the application or backend services
- Attempting to extract source code, algorithms, or proprietary methods
- Creating derivative works based on the Service without authorization
- Copying or replicating the Service's features for competing products

### 3.5 Data Misuse

- Linking bank accounts that do not belong to you
- Providing false or misleading personal information
- Using the Service to aggregate data for commercial purposes (e.g., market research, competitor analysis)
- Scraping or bulk-exporting data for unauthorized purposes

### 3.6 Harmful Content

- Transmitting malware, viruses, or other harmful code through the Service
- Using transaction notes or other user-input fields to store or transmit harmful, offensive, or illegal content
- Submitting content designed to exploit or attack AI systems (prompt injection)

### 3.7 Commercial Use

- Using the Service to provide financial services to third parties
- Reselling, sublicensing, or redistributing access to the Service
- Using the Service as part of a commercial product or offering
- Using AI-generated insights for commercial advisory services

## 4. Account Responsibilities

### 4.1 One Account Per Person

Each user should maintain only one IndusWealth account. Creating multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions or abuse features is prohibited.

### 4.2 Account Security

You are responsible for:

- Keeping your login credentials confidential
- Using a strong, unique password
- Logging out on shared or public devices
- Reporting suspected unauthorized access immediately

### 4.3 Accurate Information

You agree to provide accurate and truthful information when:

- Creating your account (email, name)
- Adding personal details (date of birth)
- Entering custom debt information (balances, APR, payment amounts)
- Linking bank accounts (must be accounts you own or are authorized to access)

## 5. Fair Use

To ensure service quality for all users:

- **API rate limits** are enforced and must be respected
- **Data sync frequency** is managed by the system - excessive manual refresh requests may be throttled
- **AI insight generation** is cached for 6 hours - repeated requests within this window will serve cached results
- The Service is designed for **individual use** - usage patterns consistent with automated or commercial access may be flagged
